# Tileable — Legal, IP, Privacy, and Monetization Policy

## Status

Approved project policy. These rules are implementation constraints for Tileable and should be treated as authoritative when designing future product features.

This document is a product-policy record, not a substitute for legal advice. Formal trademark filing and final public legal documents should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch where appropriate.

## 0A — Brand Name and Trademark Direction

- Product name: **Tileable**.
- Preliminary name clearance: proceed with Tileable.
- Formal trademark review/final filing is still recommended before public launch or significant brand investment.
- Primary trademark filing direction: **International Class 42** for the online non-downloadable software service.
- Working service concept: online non-downloadable software for tracking collections and inventories of magnetic construction toy sets and components and identifying construction designs that can be created using a user's inventory.
- Class 9 should only be considered later if Tileable genuinely offers downloadable instructions, data files, software, or similar downloadable goods.
- Class 41 should only be considered later if Tileable genuinely operates an online non-downloadable publication service.

## 0B — Manufacturer and Product Reference Rules

Tileable is an independent, manufacturer-neutral platform.

### Allowed by default

Tileable may use factual product information to identify and catalog magnetic construction toy products, including:

- Manufacturer/brand name
- Product/set name
- SKU, model, or set identifier
- Advertised piece count
- Piece breakdown
- Dimensions
- Colors
- Release information
- Compatibility observations
- Other factual product data

Tileable must maintain its **own factual database and taxonomy**. Do not copy another platform's expressive descriptions, taxonomy wholesale, curated database, presentation, or proprietary content.

### Restricted by default

Do not use without permission, license, or another clearly documented right:

- Manufacturer logos
- Official manufacturer or retailer product photography
- Packaging artwork
- Manufacturer marketing graphics
- Manufacturer-written product descriptions copied verbatim
- Manufacturer build guides or instructional images

Product photography and other media must have documented provenance and usage rights appropriate to the intended use.

### Manufacturer outreach

For brands like MAGNA-TILES, our future outreach should go through **business development, licensing, marketing, affiliate, or brand-permission channels** and should disclose only what is necessary for the request.

Do not submit Tileable or its product concept through manufacturer inventor/concept-submission programs unless specifically approved after legal review.

### Non-affiliation principle

Tileable must not present manufacturer names, marks, or product references in a way that implies sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement.

A final public non-affiliation/trademark statement should be reviewed before launch.

## 0C — User Content and DMCA

### Creator ownership

Creators retain ownership of content they submit.

Tileable should receive only the non-exclusive license needed to host, store, reproduce, display, resize, format, distribute, operate, improve, and promote the service using that submitted content.

### Uploader certification

Before public user-generated-content submission launches, uploaders must affirm that they created the submitted content or otherwise have the necessary rights to submit and license it.

Copied creator content is prohibited. Users may not upload another person's photos, instructions, paid/free build files, or other protected content and represent it as their own.

### DMCA timing

Before **public user-generated-content uploads** launch, Tileable must have:

- A registered DMCA designated agent
- Public DMCA contact information
- A copyright/DMCA policy
- A takedown workflow
- A counter-notice workflow
- A repeat-infringer policy

This is not required merely for the current private Personal Alpha if public UGC is not available.

### Attribution and Tileable originals

- Community-created builds should support creator attribution.
- Tileable-created builds should be clearly distinguishable from community-created builds, e.g. **Tileable Original**.

### Third-party IP / fan-inspired builds

- Paid third-party-IP build content is not part of the MVP.
- Fan-inspired builds may be allowed subject to platform rules, non-affiliation treatment, and takedowns.
- Tileable should not use third-party logos as category graphics or imply that fan builds are officially licensed.

## 0D — Privacy, Accounts, and COPPA

### Intended audience

Tileable should be positioned for adults, parents, educators, collectors, and builders rather than as a children's social network.

### Account age and child data

- Tileable accounts are intended for users **13 and older** for the MVP.
- Do not create accounts for children under 13 in the MVP.
- Do not build a parental-consent system unless direct under-13 use becomes a deliberate future product decision.
- Do not ask for date of birth, age, grade, school, child's name, or similar child-profile information unless a future requirement is separately reviewed.
- Do not use targeted advertising to children.

### Minimum account data

Collect only what the product actually needs. Expected account information may include:

- Email address
- Authentication data
- Nickname-style display name
- Owned sets and quantities
- Saved builds/favorites
- Creator submissions if public UGC launches later
- Necessary technical/security/session information

### Public profile rules

- Display names must be **nickname-style**, not full legal names.
- Display names must be filtered against inappropriate/prohibited words.
- No user-uploaded profile images.
- Users choose from a curated set of pre-selected Tileable avatar/profile-image options, similar to Netflix's profile-choice model.
- Never display user email publicly.
- Do not publicly expose age, birthday, school, location, or child-identifying information.

### Privacy controls

- A Privacy Policy is required before public account launch.
- Users should have a straightforward account-deletion mechanism.
- Tileable does not sell users' personal information.
- Any future advertising technology or expanded data-sharing model requires a new privacy review before activation.

## 0E — Monetization

### MVP model

Tileable's core platform should remain free during the MVP phase.

Approved monetization methods:

1. **Affiliate links**
2. **Voluntary support** in a “Buy Me a Coffee”-style model

Voluntary support should be framed as optional support for maintaining Tileable, hosting, development, and future improvements. It is not payment for access or special treatment.

### Affiliate disclosure

Affiliate relationships must be disclosed clearly near relevant shopping/recommendation links. Working disclosure concept:

> Tileable may earn a commission from purchases made through these links.

Final wording should comply with the specific affiliate program and applicable disclosure requirements.

### MVP restrictions

- No sponsored ranking bias.
- No paid placement presented as organic recommendation.
- No monetization design that undermines trust in collection matching or build recommendations.

### Later monetization

Once Tileable has meaningful traffic and visibility, revisit additional income sources. New revenue methods should receive product, privacy, and legal review before implementation rather than being assumed to fit the MVP policy automatically.
